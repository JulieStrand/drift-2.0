Concordia Residence

Over the course of nearly a decade, this backyard was transformed from a bare lawn and broken concrete slabs into a series of lush, functional outdoor spaces tailored to family life. The design and installation were completed almost entirely by the owner, with careful attention to craft and detail.
Key features include a raised vegetable garden constructed with board-formed concrete walls capped by a cedar fence, a cedar deck and concrete paver slab patio, and updated fencing for both safety and visual clarity. A stainless steel cable trellis supports Star Jasmine along the garage, adding vertical greenery and seasonal fragrance. Dappled light and the sound of rustling leaves from a new grove of aspens creates a calming hammock space, while a timber-and-gravel fi repit terrace offers a welcoming gathering area for family and friends.
Location: Portland, OR
Status: Completed 2020
