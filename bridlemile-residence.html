Bridlemile Residence

A comprehensive residential landscape redesign in Portland's Bridlemile neighborhood, focused on creating a more cohesive relationship between the home, street frontage, driveway, and backyard. The design replaces aging paving with a restrained palette of sand-finish concrete and introduces dry-stacked stone walls, basalt flagstone and gravel surfacing that reorganize the site around improved circulation, outdoor recreation, and gathering spaces. Key elements include a new driveway and entry sequence, stone retaining walls, integrated basketball court, hot tub terrace, fire-pit area, and refreshed lawn and planting areas.
The planting design builds on established trees and shrubs, weaving in a layered palette of manzanita, ceanothus, ornamental grasses, lavender, sage, yarrow, penstemon, hydrangea, and other drought-adapted and regionally appropriate species. The design creates a more welcoming and cohesive street presence, softening the home and hardscape with seasonal color, texture, and a strong Pacific Northwest character.
